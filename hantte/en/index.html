Battery

At home and at work,
it rests

Save the places you spend your days as My Places, and while you’re inside them, Hantte stops measuring where you are. It rests for most of the day, so your battery lasts. Step out the door, and the path picks up again.

On days you want every turn recorded — like a trip — switch on Travel Log. It turns itself off at midnight, and stops on its own if your battery runs low. Turning it on is always up to you.

While Travel Log is on, you can see the elapsed time and distance on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Hantte doesn’t ask for every permission up front. It asks for Photos the first time you open Days, and for Health when you turn on step counts — right at the moment you use them.

Privacy

Where you’ve been
is yours alone.

Your records stay only on your iPhone and in your iCloud. Hantte has no server, so even the person who made it has no way to look.

No account, no sign-in, no ads. No analytics, either.

Read the privacy policy

Drawing maps and turning addresses into place names uses Apple Maps. Syncing between your devices happens through your own iCloud.

You can export your records to a file in Settings and import them again.

FAQ

Questions

Won’t it drain my battery?

Inside the My Places you’ve saved, such as home or work, Hantte doesn’t measure your location at all. Out and about, it normally records only larger movements, using a low-power method.

Travel Log, which records your path in fine detail, runs only when you switch it on, and turns off at midnight.

Why does location need to be set to Always?

It lets Hantte keep tracing your path while the app is closed. You can still use it with While Using the App, but then only the path from while the app was open will be kept.

Can anyone else see where I’ve been?

No. Your records are stored only on your iPhone and in your iCloud, and are never sent to anyone, including the developer. They leave your device only when you make and share a poster yourself — and even then, the roads near home and work are hidden.

Do I need to upload or move my photos?

No. Hantte reads the photos already in Photos and shows them as they are. It doesn’t make copies, and it doesn’t change your photos unless you choose to add a location or delete one.

Can I see days from before I installed it?

Your photos, yes. Photos with a location are placed where they were taken, and you can gather them into moments from past trips. Your path is recorded from the day you start using Hantte.
